Our image solutions are powered by Deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), which provide a flexible interface to extract intricate features from images for recognition and classification. A CNN is an attempt to model a human brain's visual recognition ability, and have been shown to achieve state-of-the-art results in automatic object recognition. We provide an intuitive and easy-to-use API. Simplicity and flexibility is the design mantra behind the Markovian Labs API. A user provides an image or a collection of images, chooses one of our pre-trained model or train their own model, and build powerful visual applications.
